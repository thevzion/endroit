# Publication

## Responsibility

State the one question, definition, argument or dated observation owned here.

## Stability

Choose `living`, `release-aligned`, `dated` or `historical`, then name the
events that require a re-read.

## Intent

State the thesis, audience and intended effect.

## Dependencies

List canonical definitions separately from related reading. A dependency
triggers review; it never makes another article a mandatory prerequisite.

## Boundaries

Record claims, sources, exclusions and destination-specific constraints.

## Publication record

Keep prior revisions, approval and verified Handle links here. Reopening a
published Publication creates a draft source revision without changing the
observed Handle.
