# Archive Room material

Archive only inactive durable material after the human chooses the exact
scope.

- Home material moves below `archive/rooms/`.
- Desk material moves below `.desk/archive/rooms/`.
- Remove the Material's relative link from `Active retained Material` in the
  owning `ROOM.md`.
- Preserve exactly one durable copy and an index entry linking the former
  owner, archive date and restoration destination.
- Restoration returns material to its original scoped Room unless the
  human explicitly chooses a new owner.

Never archive active work, Temp payloads, Site sources or generated state.
