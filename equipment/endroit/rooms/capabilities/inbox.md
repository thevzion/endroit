# Maintain a Room Inbox

Use `inbox.md` only for explicitly deferred matter owned by the Room.

Each entry keeps a stable ID, concise subject, capture date, status and the
condition that should cause review. Valid statuses are `open`, `routed`,
`resolved` and `dropped`.

Do not read an Inbox during normal routing. Review it only on explicit request,
a declared trigger or a Room hygiene pass. When an entry becomes active,
link the Meeting, Study, Initiative or other durable destination rather than
duplicating its content.
