# Maintain the Workplace

Start read-only. Compose the Workplace, Member, Desk, Room and Site Doctors,
Equipment status and trust, Route/projection checks, and Material/Meeting/
decision/archive inspection. Report four separate categories:

1. confirmed inconsistencies;
2. ownership ambiguities;
3. legacy residue;
4. optional improvements.

Every advisory must name its evidence and why it matters. Look for misplaced
instructions, overloaded or duplicate documents, Rooms that have become too
broad, orphaned Material and ambiguous delivery destinations.

Never repair from `maintain`. A repair requires one exact finding ID and a
matching explicit approval, uses an existing Endroit operation, then reruns
the inspection. The only Git topology repair is the exact
`checkout-index-stale` finding through `checkout reconcile --apply`; adoption,
deletion, parking, supersession and submodule operations remain explicit.
Never move, delete, archive, push or deliver implicitly. This
Equipment creates no daemon, schedule, hidden memory or periodic cleanup.
