# Planning roadmap

Maintain one `planning/roadmap.md` per Workspace, created only when the
Workspace has an Initiative to link.

Every row links a canonical Initiative Artifact. Raw ideas remain in the
Workspace Inbox. Put `active` and `blocked` in Now, `ready` in Next, `draft` in
Later, and `complete` or `cancelled` in History.

Do not copy tickets, PRD, SPEC or handoff into the roadmap.
