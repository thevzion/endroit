# Research Study

Create or continue one bounded, evidence-backed Study after resolving the Home
or Desk Workspace that owns its question.

Use one canonical document at
`<workspace>/researching/studies/<slug>/study.md`. Create it only when the
question and result are durable enough to reuse; an isolated lookup does not
need a Study.

Keep one bounded question. Prefer current primary sources, date every
observation and distinguish confirmed facts, inferences and unknowns. Record
useful negative coverage when searched channels constrain the conclusion.

Persist question, scope, current answer, findings, timeline when relevant,
evidence, coverage, gaps, Workspace implications and revalidation triggers.
Use `active`, `current`, `stale` or `archived` as status.

A Study is a Document, not an Artifact. Planning and Publishing may cite it by
link or `derived_from`, but Research never creates a plan or publication
automatically.

Never persist a transcript, hidden reasoning, credentials, raw browsing log or
copied third-party page.
