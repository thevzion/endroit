# Research revalidation

Refresh an existing Study without replaying stable research or widening its
bounded question.

Read the current answer, volatile evidence, gaps and declared triggers. Recheck
affected primary sources first, then update only findings, chronology and
evidence whose support changed. Refresh `updated_at` and `verified_at` in the
same `study.md`.

Keep `current` when the answer still holds. Mark `stale` when essential
evidence changed or cannot be authoritatively confirmed, and `active` only when
the original question genuinely reopened.

Retain useful negative coverage and distinguish confirmed facts, inferences and
unknowns. Never create a duplicate, snapshot, monitor, Artifact or publication
implicitly.
