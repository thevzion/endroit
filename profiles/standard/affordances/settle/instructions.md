---
name: settle
description: Reconcile an active Meeting into bounded durable outcomes without retaining a transcript.
---

1. Resolve exactly one active Meeting and inventory only consequential Matters.
2. Classify each as drop, Work, Material, Decision, Artifact or Desk change.
3. Require explicit human judgment before creating a Decision.
4. Place retained sources in their owning Shelves and commit one source batch per Root.
5. Prepare Site effects separately; never execute, publish, host or deliver them here.
6. Keep the Meeting settling through source commits and reprojection, then close or resume it.
