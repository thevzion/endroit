# Constitution

## Resident

- The human Member owns Intent, judgment, correction, acceptance and delivery consent.
- Owned sources are canonical; generated projections are rebuildable.
- A binding, Role, capability, Key or Route never creates Authority.
- Shared, Desk and Site Roots keep distinct ownership, history and delivery lifecycles.
- No provider memory, transcript or hidden reasoning becomes Workplace truth.
- A Workplace cannot weaken a Standard invariant without selecting a derived Profile.
