# Lexicon

- **Agent** (`agent`) — A durable non-human identity only when independently owned.
- **Desk** (`desk`) — A private Member-indexed Place with a bound Root.
- **Material** (`material`) — Retained evidence, knowledge or contract.
- **Meeting** (`meeting`) — A present collaboration event with Occupants and disposition.
- **Member** (`member`) — A human owner of judgment and consent.
- **Place** (`place`) — A governed addressable location or sovereignty boundary.
- **Room** (`room`) — A Place owning one durable semantic domain.
- **Shelf** (`shelf`) — A typed Placement contract inside a Place.
- **Site** (`site`) — A Workplace declaration of a sovereign external Root.
- **Work** (`work`) — A commitment to a bounded durable Outcome.
- **Workbench** (`workbench`) — A reconstructible projection of the Items needed now.
- **Workshop** (`workshop`) — A situated resolution of Work, Place, Equipment and Authority.
- **Workspace** (`workspace`) — A Place used for a bounded responsibility.
