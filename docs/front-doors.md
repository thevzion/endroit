# Front Doors

A Front Door is pre-action complete, not knowledge complete. It tells an Agent
where it is, whose sources it sees, what authority it does not have and which
local destinations are legitimate next.

The compiler derives one provider-neutral FrontDoor IR and renders ordinary
files:

- FRONTDOOR.md for the provider-neutral local Mount representation;
- AGENTS.md for Codex discovery;
- CLAUDE.md for Claude discovery.

Neutral entry contains onboarding only. Bound entry contains the declared
Member/Desk card and exact WELCOME disclosure. “Bound” is a static association,
not detection of the current human and never an Authority grant.

Constitution, Doctrine and CHANGE remain complete editable sources and each
owns one explicit `## Resident` section. The Hall copies those sections into a
single resident operating contract because they govern the first routing
decision. The Profile applies one aggregate byte budget and compilation fails
rather than truncating or substituting a summary.

Source Ref, revision, visibility and disclosure reason remain in FrontDoor IR,
the Disclosure Contract and HTML provenance comments. They are not repeated as
visible policy cards. MEMORY remains summary plus link until its Intent applies;
WELCOME remains full and independently bounded.

Scoped entries progressively reveal Room, Meeting, Workshop, method and Workbench
surfaces. A parent links to a child contract rather than copying it.

Coordination follows the same rule. The Hall carries only Main's bounded
facilitation summary. A Work may link the Manager contract for integration; a
Site links the Worker contract only for a bounded dispatch.

A fresh bound Hall has no Room but remains actionable through its local
`methods/open-room.md`. That method is loaded only when an intention needs a
new continuity boundary. The resulting Room then reveals its own methods.

The Hall also carries the compact pre-write resolution matrix. Critical rules
are never left behind an optional link: they are resident, required by the
applicable method, or enforced by the public guards. Each placement retains its
source revision and reason in the compiled ControlClause.

Portable maps and neutral Views live in the shared Root's `.workplace/`.
Provider discovery files live only at the Mount and are explicit
ProviderBinding targets.
