# Workplace anatomy

```text
Workplace
├── Hall: entry, Reception, Floor Plan, Member Card, Keyring
├── Constitution: invariants and Authority
├── Doctrine: default judgment
├── Member directory
├── Desk Roots: identity, WELCOME and private Shelves
├── Rooms: durable semantic domains
│   ├── Shelves: typed durable placement
│   ├── Meetings: present collaboration events and bounded contributions
│   ├── Workspace: Place + current Work + ownership boundary
│   ├── Workshop: applicable Equipment for that Work
│   ├── Workbench: reconstructed working set
│   └── Noticeboard: Views, Register and Ledger
└── Routes: addresses to sovereign Sites
```

Place, Member, Agent, Work, Material and Meeting are the durable families. The rest is a
closed Role, relation, Slot, affordance or projection unless independent
identity, ownership, lifecycle, authority or history proves otherwise.

Places contain Shelves. Shelves hold addressable Items. Workbenches assemble the
Items needed now.
